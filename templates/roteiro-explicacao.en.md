# Feynman Out-Loud Explanation Script (Show Stage)

1. **What it is** (one single sentence, zero jargon).
2. **What it is used for**, with a concrete real-world scenario.
3. **How it works under the hood**, in 3 clean steps.
4. **When to use it** and when **NOT** to use it.
5. **Common confusions** (what it is easily mistaken for).
6. **Exam question angle:** how could the certification test this concept?

> *If you stumble or hesitate on any item, return to your study material and record it again.*
