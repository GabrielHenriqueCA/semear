# Prompt: build my SEMEAR cycle

```
I want to study for [CERTIFICATION] with the SEMEAR method (Sow, Study, Show, Exercise,
Assess, Review). I have [X] hours per week and the exam is on [DATE].
My preferred study format is [video/reading/hands-on].

Build a week by week schedule based on the domains of the official guide, reserving:
- 1 Sow phase at the start, followed by a pause of a few days,
- Show and Exercise at the end of each module,
- one mock exam per week,
- 10 to 15 minutes of daily spaced review.
Be realistic and leave slack for the unexpected.
```
