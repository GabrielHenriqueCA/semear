```
I just finished studying the [MODULE] module of the [CERTIFICATION] certification.
Generate 10 multiple choice questions in the style of the exam, with realistic scenarios.

Rules:
- Mix difficulty levels: 4 easy, 4 medium, 2 hard.
- Include plausible distractors (similar services, common traps).
- Do NOT show the answer key. Wait until I answer all of them.
- After I answer, grade them one by one, explain why the right answer is right
  and why each wrong answer is wrong.
- For each of my mistakes, classify the cause: concept, confusing similar services,
  misreading, or memory. This goes into my error log.
- If you are not sure about a fact, tell me to check it in the official documentation.
```
